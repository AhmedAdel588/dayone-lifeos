When you're having a difficult day

Try a 2-minute reset

Pause, take a few slow comfortable breaths, drink some water, and name one thing you can do next.

Make the next step smaller

Choose one achievable action: a short walk, a simple meal, messaging someone you trust, or writing down what's bothering you. If distress is persistent or severe, consider professional support.
